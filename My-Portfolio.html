EXPLAINABLE FEDERATED FRAMEWORK FOR ENHANCED SECURITY AND PRIVACY IN CONNECTED VEHICLES AGAINST ADVANCED PERSISTENT THREATS

The project “Explainable Federated Framework for Enhanced Security and Privacy in Connected Vehicles against Advanced Persistent Threats” focuses on improving cybersecurity in IoT-enabled vehicular systems. It uses federated learning to train models locally on vehicles without sharing sensitive data, ensuring privacy. The framework integrates deep neural networks for accurate detection of advanced persistent threats (APTs), which are complex and long-term cyberattacks. Additionally, Explainable AI techniques are used to provide transparency in decision-making. According to the abstract in your report , the system achieves high detection accuracy while maintaining scalability, security, and data privacy in distributed vehicle environments.
